## Evidence classification
- **Observed fact:** directly observed in a stated environment or source.
- **Approved model:** assertion in an authoritative, approved model.
- **Repository-declared:** statement made by a repository or document, not independently verified.
- **Inference:** conclusion derived from other evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** proposition requiring validation.
- **Proposed target:** intended future state, not current reality.
- **Unresolved gap:** identity, relationship or evidence not yet established.
